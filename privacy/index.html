Privacy

Privacy

The Great Brandomizer is a free tool run by Harrison Greener. This page explains what information is used when you visit it.

Last updated 24 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your saved spins and your sound setting are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They're never sent to me or to any server, and clearing your browser data deletes them.

There are no accounts, and nothing you type into the machine, such as your brand name, leaves your browser.

Services the site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google Fonts' privacy FAQ.
  • jsDelivr serves the three.js 3D engine that draws the machine. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. As the site itself doesn't collect personal information, most requests will be about the services above. You can contact me through my website.
