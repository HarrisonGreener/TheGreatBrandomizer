Editorial colour palette and fonts

Editorial design borrows from magazines and newspapers: elegant serif headlines, crisp sans serif or serif body text, clear hierarchy and a restrained palette of ink, newsprint and one strong colour such as a deep red or navy. It's built for reading and makes words look important.

Where the Editorial look works

Publishers, journalism, blogs and newsletters, cultural institutions, bookshops and thought-leadership brands.

Tips for using it

  • Make the headline serif large and the body text comfortable to read, around 17 to 20 pixels on screen.
  • Use the accent colour like a magazine would: for kickers, pull quotes and section labels.
