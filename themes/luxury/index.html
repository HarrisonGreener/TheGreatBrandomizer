Luxury colour palette and fonts

Luxury branding is quiet confidence. Deep navy, forest green or burgundy grounds are paired with gold, champagne and soft taupe. Type is refined and classical: high-contrast serifs, thin capitals and generous letter-spacing. Nothing shouts, because it doesn't need to.

Where the Luxury look works

Jewellery, fashion houses, hotels, fine dining, property, perfume and premium spirits.

Tips for using it

  • Letter-space small capitals widely for labels and navigation. It's one of the quickest ways to look expensive.
  • Keep gold for details like rules, logos and small type. Large areas of gold start to look cheap.
