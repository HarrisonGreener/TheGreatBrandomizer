Japandi colour palette and fonts

Japandi blends Japanese wabi-sabi with Scandinavian simplicity. It's warm minimalism: rice paper whites, oak and clay, sage, charcoal and indigo. Type is quiet and refined, often a delicate serif paired with a soft sans serif, with lots of breathing room around everything.

Where the Japandi look works

Ceramics and homeware, tea and coffee, skincare, yoga and wellness, architecture and interiors.

Tips for using it

  • Use texture, like paper grain or linen, instead of extra colours to add depth.
  • Leave more empty space than feels comfortable. Restraint is the whole idea.
