Minimal colour palette and fonts

Minimal design strips everything back to what's needed: a near-white background, a near-black for text, a few greys and one colour that does all the talking. Typography is simple and well spaced, and empty space is treated as part of the design rather than something to fill.

Of the 21 colour pairs in this palette, 3 pass WCAG AAA and 5 pass AA for body text. These are the strongest pairs for text:

  • Aa9.81:1 AAA#141414 on #BDBDBD

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Minimal look works

Architecture, photography, skincare, fashion, product design and personal portfolios.

Tips for using it

  • Use the accent colour once per screen or page. Its rarity is what makes it powerful.
  • Rely on size and weight for hierarchy instead of extra colours or decoration.
