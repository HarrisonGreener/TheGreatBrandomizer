Contrast check

Of the 21 colour pairs in this palette, 4 pass WCAG AAA and 7 pass AA for body text. These are the strongest pairs for text:

  • Aa11.89:1 AAA#2C3442 on #F8F9FB

  • Aa4.44:1 AA large#2C3442 on #FF6B4A

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Modern look works

Startups, apps, SaaS products, agencies, consultancies and any brand that wants to feel current and trustworthy.

Tips for using it

  • Pick one accent and use it for buttons and links only. The rest of the palette should stay quiet.
  • Generous spacing is what makes this look premium. When in doubt, add more white space.
