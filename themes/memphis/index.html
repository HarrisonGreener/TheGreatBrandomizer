Memphis colour palette and fonts

Memphis was a design group founded in Milan in 1981 by Ettore Sottsass. It threw out good taste on purpose: furniture and prints covered in squiggles, terrazzo speckles, zigzags and black-and-white grids, in clashing pinks, yellows, turquoise and cobalt. The shapes are simple and chunky, and the whole look is cheerful, loud and a little bit silly.

Where the Memphis look works

Streetwear, snack and drinks brands, toy and homeware shops, events, creative studios and playful apps.

Tips for using it

  • Pick two or three simple patterns, like a squiggle, a speckle and a grid, and repeat them in your palette colours.
  • Black and white patterns next to bright flat shapes are what make it Memphis rather than just colourful.
