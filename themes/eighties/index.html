Eighties colour palette and fonts

The Eighties theme leans into arcade cabinets, synth album covers and neon signage. Expect hot pink, electric cyan, ultraviolet and sunset orange on deep, almost black backgrounds. Type is chrome-flavoured, italic, outlined or pixel-based, built to look fast and bright.

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Eighties look works

Gaming, esports, nightlife, retro events, energy drinks, synthwave music and anything that wants to feel loud and electric.

Tips for using it

  • Neon only glows on dark. Keep the background deep navy or violet and let the bright colours sit on top.
  • Check contrast carefully. Pink on purple looks great but often fails for body text, so keep small copy in the lightest shade.
