Western colour palette and fonts

The Western theme is the wanted poster and the old frontier general store: slab serif woodtype, Tuscan letters with split serifs, sun-bleached paper, rust, saddle tan, denim blue and turquoise. It feels sturdy, handmade and full of character.

Contrast check

Of the 21 colour pairs in this palette, 2 pass WCAG AAA and 5 pass AA for body text. These are the strongest pairs for text:

  • Aa14.15:1 AAA#231710 on #F3E6CC
  • Aa7.33:1 AAA#231710 on #D2A02E
  • Aa6.83:1 AA#231710 on #C59A6A
  • Aa5.63:1 AA#3D5B82 on #F3E6CC
  • Aa4.78:1 AA#A8461E on #F3E6CC
  • Aa4.49:1 AA large#231710 on #2F8F86
  • Aa3.15:1 AA large#2F8F86 on #F3E6CC

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Western look works

BBQ joints, whiskey and bourbon, denim and workwear, country music, rodeos, barbers and outdoor brands.

Tips for using it

  • Stack several slab serif lines of different sizes and widths, like a woodtype poster.
  • Use the tan and paper shades as backgrounds so the rust and denim tones stand out.
