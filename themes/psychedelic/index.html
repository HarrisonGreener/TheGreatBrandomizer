Psychedelic colour palette and fonts

Psychedelic design is the late-1960s gig poster: lettering that melts and bends to fill a shape, vibrating colour combinations and swirling patterns. Colours fight on purpose, with magenta next to orange and acid green next to violet. Type is decorative and often hard to read, which was part of the fun.

Where the Psychedelic look works

Music festivals, gig posters, craft beer, vintage shops and anything that wants to feel bold and trippy.

Tips for using it

  • Put complementary colours side by side at similar brightness to get that vibrating effect, but keep body text on a calm background.
  • Bend headline lettering to fit a shape, like a circle or a wave, for an authentic period feel.
