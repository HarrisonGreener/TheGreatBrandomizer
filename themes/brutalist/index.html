Brutalist colour palette and fonts

Brutalist design takes its name from raw concrete architecture. On screen and in print it means exposed structure, default-looking type, heavy borders, harsh contrast and layouts that feel deliberately unpolished. The palette is black, white and grey with a single loud colour such as safety red, yellow or blue.

Where the Brutalist look works

Art collectives, fashion labels, independent magazines, developer tools, portfolios and brands that want to feel honest and unfiltered.

Tips for using it

  • Show the grid and the borders. Brutalism celebrates the structure instead of hiding it.
  • Monospaced and grotesque fonts at large sizes do most of the heavy lifting.
