Cyberpunk colour palette and fonts

Cyberpunk is the neon-soaked future of films, games and comics: rain-slick city streets, glitchy screens and warning labels. The palette is near-black with acid yellow, cyan, magenta and alarm red. Type is angular, technical and often monospaced, like a heads-up display.

Contrast check

Of the 21 colour pairs in this palette, 4 pass WCAG AAA and 7 pass AA for body text. These are the strongest pairs for text:

  • Aa16.22:1 AAA#0A0B14 on #FCEE0A

  • Aa10.65:1 AAA#2A3148 on #FCEE0A

  • Aa5.29:1 AA#0A0B14 on #FF2A45
  • Aa4.78:1 AA#7A2CFF on #FCEE0A
  • Aa4.03:1 AA large#2A3148 on #FF2BD6

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Cyberpunk look works

Gaming, esports, electronic music, tech events, energy drinks and sci-fi projects.

Tips for using it

  • Put yellow and black stripes or warning labels in your graphics for an instant cyberpunk cue.
  • Let one neon colour dominate each layout. Using all of them equally turns into noise.
