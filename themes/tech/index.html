AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Tech look works

SaaS products, AI tools, developer platforms, fintech, data products and hackathons.

Tips for using it

  • Use a monospaced font for small details like labels, numbers and code. It instantly signals tech.
  • Glow the accent colour slightly on dark backgrounds, but keep reading text a soft off-white rather than pure white.
