Risograph colour palette and fonts

Risograph is a print technique that layers bright soy inks one colour at a time, so prints come out slightly misaligned with a grainy, hand-made texture. Designers love its fluorescent pink, bright blue, sunflower yellow and teal on warm paper.

Where the Risograph look works

Zines, independent publishers, illustrators, gig posters, cafes and creative studios.

Tips for using it

  • Fake the misregistration by offsetting a second colour layer by a few pixels and setting it to multiply.
  • Keep to two or three ink colours per design, as a real Riso print would.
