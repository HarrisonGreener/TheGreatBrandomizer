Punk Zine colour palette and fonts

Punk zines were made with scissors, glue sticks and a photocopier. The look is ransom-note lettering, torn paper, marker scrawl and high-contrast black and white, with fluorescent pink and yellow added for shock. Nothing lines up, and that's the point.

Contrast check

Of the 21 colour pairs in this palette, 3 pass WCAG AAA and 5 pass AA for body text. These are the strongest pairs for text:

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Punk Zine look works

Bands and gigs, skate brands, independent shops, activist campaigns, tattoo studios and zines.

Tips for using it

  • Mix one marker-style headline with a typewriter body font for an instant cut-and-paste feel.
  • Rotate elements slightly and let them overlap, as if they were stuck down by hand.
