Seventies colour palette and fonts

Seventies design is warm, earthy and easy-going. It's the decade of burnt orange kitchens, mustard sofas, avocado bathrooms and chunky, soft-cornered lettering. The palette sits in the browns, oranges and olive greens, with a creamy off-white to lift it. Typography is heavy and rounded, with fat serifs and tight letter-spacing.

Where the Seventies look works

Coffee, bakeries, vinyl and audio brands, plant shops, hospitality and lifestyle brands that want warmth and nostalgia.

Tips for using it

  • Stack colours in stripes. Three or four bands of orange, mustard and brown are an instant Seventies signal.
  • Tight letter-spacing on bold display type is what gives it that period feel. Don't open it up too much.
