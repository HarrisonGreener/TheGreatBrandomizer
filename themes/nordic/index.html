Nordic colour palette and fonts

Nordic design is calm, natural and functional. It uses soft fjord blues, mist grey, birch and oat tones, moss green and a touch of clay. Type is clean, light and friendly, and layouts rely on plenty of space and natural light rather than decoration.

Where the Nordic look works

Interiors, homeware, wellbeing, outdoor brands, sustainable products and cafes.

Tips for using it

  • Keep contrast gentle in large areas and save the darkest shade for text.
  • Rounded, airy sans serifs suit this style better than sharp, tight ones.
