Swiss Style colour palette and fonts

Swiss Style, also called the International Typographic Style, came from 1950s Switzerland and is still the backbone of modern graphic design. It's built on strict grids, flush-left sans serif type, lots of white space and objective photography. The palette is mostly black, white and grey, with a single bold red.

Where the Swiss Style look works

Corporate identities, transport and signage, architecture, museums, annual reports and tech products.

Tips for using it

  • Set up a grid first and put everything on it. The discipline is the style.
  • Use red sparingly, for one key word or shape, so it has full impact.
