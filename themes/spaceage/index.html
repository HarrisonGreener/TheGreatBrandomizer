Space Age colour palette and fonts

Space Age design came from the 1960s race to the moon, when chrome, white plastic and rocket-launch orange appeared on everything from radios to architecture. It looks to the future the way people imagined it back then: rounded rectangles, wide extended type, and clean, technical layouts with numbers and countdowns. The palette is cool silver and navy with bold signal orange and red.

Where the Space Age look works

Tech hardware, festivals, sci-fi games, observatories, synth and electronic music, and launch campaigns that want a sense of occasion.

Tips for using it

  • Wide, extended typefaces carry this style. Give them generous letter-spacing and set them in capitals.
  • Treat orange as a signal colour. One orange element on a navy or silver layout says more than several.
