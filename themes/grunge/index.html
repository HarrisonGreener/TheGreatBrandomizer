Grunge colour palette and fonts

Grunge design is the 1990s alternative scene on paper: distressed type, photocopy texture, stamped and typewritten lettering and muddy, worn-in colours like olive, rust, oxblood and nicotine yellow. It's deliberately rough and rejects polish.

Where the Grunge look works

Bands, skate and streetwear, tattoo studios, independent coffee and record shops, and gritty event posters.

Tips for using it

  • Add texture, like grain, scratches or photocopy noise, over the colours. Flat fills look too clean for grunge.
  • Typewriter and stencil fonts give you authenticity without much effort.
