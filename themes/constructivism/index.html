Constructivism colour palette and fonts

Constructivism came out of 1920s Russia, where artists used design as propaganda for a new society. The posters are all diagonals, photomontage, bold red blocks and heavy condensed type that seems to shout. The palette is red, black and aged paper, sometimes with khaki or steel grey.

Where the Constructivism look works

Protest and campaign work, gyms, music posters, bold editorial covers and brands with a strong point of view.

Tips for using it

  • Tilt things. A layout set at 15 or 30 degrees instantly feels Constructivist.
  • Condensed, all-caps headlines at large sizes give you that urgent, poster-on-the-wall voice.
