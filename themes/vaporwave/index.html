Vaporwave colour palette and fonts

Vaporwave is an internet art style from the early 2010s that remixes 1980s and 1990s consumer culture: shopping malls, early 3D graphics, Roman busts, palm trees and Windows-era interfaces. The palette is pastel neon, with pink, teal, lavender and peach on deep indigo.

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Vaporwave look works

Music, streetwear, online creators, events, merch and anything with an ironic retro edge.

Tips for using it

  • Use gradients between the pink, peach and teal shades for skies and backgrounds.
  • Pixel and wide futuristic fonts both work. Add wide letter-spacing for the full aesthetic.
