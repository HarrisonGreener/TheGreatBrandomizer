Bauhaus colour palette and fonts

The Bauhaus was a German art school that ran from 1919 to 1933 and changed design for good. Its posters use circles, squares and triangles, strong diagonals and sans serif type with no decoration. The palette is primary red, yellow and blue with black, grey and a warm off-white. Everything serves a purpose, and that's the beauty of it.

Where the Bauhaus look works

Architects, furniture makers, galleries, design studios, education and any brand that wants to look rational and confident.

Tips for using it

  • Build the layout from basic shapes in the primary colours, then let the type sit inside or across them.
  • Geometric sans serifs are the right call. Avoid anything with flourishes.
