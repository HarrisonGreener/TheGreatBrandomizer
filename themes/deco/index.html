Art Deco colour palette and fonts

Art Deco arrived in the 1920s and 1930s with sunbursts, stepped shapes, geometric symmetry and gold everywhere. It was the style of grand hotels, cinemas and ocean liners. The palette is black and gold with deep teal, oxblood and blush, and the type is tall, elegant and geometric, often with thin strokes and dramatic capitals.

Where the Art Deco look works

Cocktail bars, hotels, events and weddings, jewellery, cinemas and luxury packaging.

Tips for using it

  • Gold reads best on black or very dark teal. Use it for lines, frames and key words rather than large fills.
  • Strong symmetry is part of the look. Centre your layout and repeat shapes on both sides.
