Y2K colour palette and fonts

Y2K design is the turn-of-the-millennium look: translucent plastic, chrome bubbles, iridescent gradients and the feeling of logging on. Palettes mix icy lilac, cyber cyan, hot magenta and lime with soft silver. Type is wide, techy and futuristic, often letter-spaced to fill a line.

Where the Y2K look works

Fashion, beauty, music artists, tech-flavoured lifestyle brands, Gen Z products and digital collectibles.

Tips for using it

  • Use the soft silver and lilac shades as large areas, then add cyan or magenta in small, shiny accents.
  • Extended display type works best in short words. Keep long reading copy in the plainer body font.
