Mid-Century colour palette and fonts

Mid-century modern covers roughly 1945 to 1965, when designers mixed post-war optimism with clean, organic shapes. Think boomerang patterns, starbursts, airline posters and record sleeves. The colours are warm but slightly muted: teal, mustard, burnt orange, olive and walnut brown, the shades of furniture and printed fabric from the period. Type is friendly and geometric, often set loose and playful.

Where the Mid-Century look works

Coffee shops, furniture and interiors brands, record labels, cocktail bars, travel companies and podcasts with a retro feel.

Tips for using it

  • Muted is the point. If a colour looks too fresh, pull its saturation down a touch so it feels printed rather than on screen.
  • Use the walnut or deep brown as text instead of black. It keeps the whole palette warm.
