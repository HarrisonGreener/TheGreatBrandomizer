Kawaii colour palette and fonts

Kawaii means cute in Japanese, and this style is soft, round and sweet: pastel pink, mint, lilac, butter yellow and baby blue, with a deep plum instead of black for text. Type is chubby and rounded, and layouts often include little characters and stickers.

Where the Kawaii look works

Stationery, toys, sweets and bubble tea, beauty, kids' apps and character-led brands.

Tips for using it

  • Pastels have very low contrast against white, so always put text in the dark plum rather than a pastel.
  • Rounded fonts and rounded corners everywhere keep the look consistent.
