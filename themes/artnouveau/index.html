Art Nouveau colour palette and fonts

Art Nouveau ran from about 1890 to 1910 and took its forms from nature: vines, flowers, flowing hair and the famous whiplash curve. Posters from the period frame figures in ornamental borders and hand-drawn lettering. The palette is soft and organic, with sage, mustard gold, plum, teal and dusty rose on warm cream.

Where the Art Nouveau look works

Florists, perfumers, tea rooms, jewellery, wellness and spa brands, theatres and boutique wine labels.

Tips for using it

  • Use curved frames and borders in the gold or sage tone to hold the layout together.
  • Choose one decorative serif for headings and keep the text face classical. The ornament should live in the shapes, not in every word.
