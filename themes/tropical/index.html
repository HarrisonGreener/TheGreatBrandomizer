Tropical colour palette and fonts

The Tropical theme is fresh, juicy and sunny: palm green, coral, mango, turquoise sea and hibiscus pink on warm cream. Type is round, bouncy and fun, sometimes script, always cheerful. It's a holiday in a palette.

Contrast check

Of the 21 colour pairs in this palette, 2 pass WCAG AAA and 4 pass AA for body text. These are the strongest pairs for text:

  • Aa11.49:1 AAA#0F3B33 on #FFF6DC
  • Aa7.07:1 AAA#0F3B33 on #FFB627
  • Aa4.87:1 AA#0F3B33 on #0FB5B0
  • Aa4.54:1 AA#0F3B33 on #FF6F5E
  • Aa4.02:1 AA large#1E8A5A on #FFF6DC
  • Aa3.49:1 AA large#E8457C on #FFF6DC
  • Aa3.29:1 AA large#0F3B33 on #E8457C

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Tropical look works

Drinks and smoothies, beach bars, holidays and travel, summer festivals, swimwear and sun care.

Tips for using it

  • Use the deep green as your anchor for text and the bright fruit colours for shapes and highlights.
  • Round, bubbly fonts suit this look. Keep sharp, serious type out of it.
