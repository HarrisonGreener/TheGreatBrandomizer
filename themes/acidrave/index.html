Acid Rave colour palette and fonts

Acid rave is the look of late-1980s and 1990s UK acid house: the yellow smiley face, warehouse party flyers, fluorescent inks and chunky, stretched or glitched type on black. It was made fast and cheap for nights out, so it's bright, bold and slightly chaotic.

Where the Acid Rave look works

Club nights, festivals, DJs and record labels, streetwear, energy drinks and merch.

Tips for using it

  • Smiley yellow on black is the core pairing. Add one more fluoro colour, not all of them at once.
  • Stretch, slant or glitch the headline type, and keep the details like dates and venues in a plain mono font, as real flyers did.
