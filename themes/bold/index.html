Contrast check

Of the 21 colour pairs in this palette, 3 pass WCAG AAA and 6 pass AA for body text. These are the strongest pairs for text:

  • Aa18.62:1 AAA#0D0D0D on #FAFAFA
  • Aa13.58:1 AAA#0D0D0D on #FFD400
  • Aa7.47:1 AAA#0D0D0D on #00B86B
  • Aa6.39:1 AA#0D0D0D on #FF4FA3
  • Aa6.01:1 AA#0047FF on #FAFAFA
  • Aa5.26:1 AA#0D0D0D on #FF2E2E
  • Aa4.39:1 AA large#0047FF on #FFD400
  • Aa3.54:1 AA large#FF2E2E on #FAFAFA

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1. How colour contrast works.

Where the Bold look works

Sportswear, fitness, campaigns, sales and promotions, events and challenger brands.

Tips for using it

  • Use full-bleed blocks of colour behind huge headlines rather than small accents.
  • Condensed display faces let you fit big words in narrow spaces, which is exactly what this style needs.
