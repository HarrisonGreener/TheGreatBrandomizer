Organic colour palette and fonts

The Organic theme takes its cues from nature: sage and moss greens, terracotta, sand, stone and sky. Type is soft and friendly, often a characterful serif with a rounded sans serif for body text. It feels handmade, grounded and good for you.

Where the Organic look works

Food and farm brands, plant shops, sustainable fashion, wellness, natural skincare and charities.

Tips for using it

  • Pair the palette with natural textures and hand-drawn marks to keep it from feeling corporate.
  • Use the terracotta as your accent. It warms up the greens and draws the eye.
