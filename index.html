How to play

  • Click the handle or START to spin. Space works too.
  • HOLD under any reel keeps it on the next spin.
  • Tap a theme on the chart at the top to switch, or press THEME to cycle. COLOURS picks 5, 6 or 7 reels.
  • Machine, Reels and Tray change the view. V cycles them.
  • CASH OUT prints your brand card into the tray. Click the card for the full payout.
  • Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom toward what you're pointing at, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan. Double-click empty space to reset.
