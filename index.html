The Great Brandomizer

Pick a design style, pull the handle of a vintage fruit machine, and spin a brand colour palette and Google Fonts pairing to match. Hold what you like, check the contrast, then cash out your brand card.

Spin Browse 38 themes

How it works

  1. Pick a theme. Choose from 38 styles in the Themes menu or on the machine's chart.
  2. Pull the handle. Five to seven colour reels, a headline font and a body font spin and land.
  3. Hold what you like. Press HOLD under any reel to keep it, then spin again to change the rest.
  4. Cash out. Get a brand card with HEX codes, a contrast check, CSS variables, a font embed and a PNG download.
