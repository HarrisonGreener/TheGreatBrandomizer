About the Brandomizer

The Great Brandomizer is a free tool for designers and anyone building a brand. Pick a style, pull the handle of a vintage fruit machine, and it spins up a colour palette and a Google Fonts pairing that suit that style, with the contrast checked for you.

Why it exists

Most palette generators are pure luck. You get five random colours that might work together but have nothing to do with the brand you're making. The Brandomizer starts from a theme instead. Each of its 38 themes, from Victorian to Cyberpunk, has its own colour rules and its own pool of fonts, so every spin stays inside a look that works. Then it adds the fun part: real reels, a big chrome handle and a printed brand card that drops into the tray.

How it works

  • Choose a theme from the Themes menu or the chart on the machine.
  • Pull the handle to spin 5, 6 or 7 colour reels, a headline font and a body font.
  • Hold the reels you like and spin again to change the rest.
  • Cash out for a brand card with HEX, RGB and HSL codes, a contrast check, CSS variables, a Google Fonts embed and a PNG or JPEG download.

Who made it

The Brandomizer was designed and made by Harrison Greener, a designer and developer working across branding, web and print. It's built with three.js and runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. The Brandomizer is free to use, with no account needed.
Can I use the colours and fonts in commercial work?
Yes. Colours can't be owned, so the palettes are yours to use. The fonts all come from Google Fonts, and almost all of them use the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial use. Check each font's licence on Google Fonts to be sure.
Where are my saved spins kept?
Saved spins are stored in your own browser on your device. They aren't sent to a server, so clearing your browser data removes them.
Does it work on phones?
Yes. It's designed for desktop first, but it works on phones and tablets too. Pinch to zoom, and use the Reels view to see the reels up close.
Why did my spin land on a "house pick"?
That's the jackpot. About one spin in forty, when nothing is held, the reels land on a hand-picked palette and font pairing for the theme.
